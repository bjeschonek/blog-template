# How to Grow a Chia Pet without Any Water

How do you get your chia pet to grow without water? Simply use hand soap. Take some hand soap you have lying around your house and douse your chia pet in it and watch it grow! 

# Steps
1. Find hand soap
2. Lather hand soap on chia pet
3. Repeat 3x per day for best results
